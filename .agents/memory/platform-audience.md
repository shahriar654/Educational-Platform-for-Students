The platform is for NDC Humanities G–L students to share class-note photos by class day across Bangla, English, ICT, Economics, History, Civics, and Geography.

**Why:** The user defined this audience and subject scope for the platform.

**How to apply:** Preserve the student audience, seven subjects, and day-based note sharing when extending this app.
